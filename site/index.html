How the door works

Based stays good by staying small and real. The rules are the same for everyone.

  1. Get a code or join the lineFrom a member, a partner venue, or the waitlist.
  2. Spots open in dropsEach city has a member cap. When spots open, the front of the line gets in first.
  3. Prove you're youReal photos, checked against a live selfie. 18+ only.
  4. Show upTalk, meet, go places. People who ghost fade out; people who show up get seen.

Questions

What is Based?

Based is one app for dating and a real social life: people to date, groups to join, events, a feed worth reading, and the bars and spots worth going to. It's members-only and 18+.

Where is Based available?

Based is opening in Calgary first. Anyone can join the waitlist for their city, and new cities open when enough people sign up.

How do I get in?

With an invite code from a member or a partner venue, or through the waitlist. Each city has a member cap and new spots open in timed drops. A member's code moves you to the front of the line.

Is Based free?

Yes, Based is free to join and use. An optional Premium membership with extras is coming.

How is Based different from other dating apps?

Photos are checked against a live selfie, contact info stays hidden until you've both sent a voice note, and matches that never talk fade out. Dating sits alongside friends, groups and events, so you meet people the way people actually meet.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not in the App Store yet. On iPhone, open based-social.com/app in Safari and add it to your home screen; it works like an app. Android members can download the app here.
