Questions

What is Based?

Based is a members-only social network for real people: a feed worth reading, groups to join, events, and the bars and spots worth going to. Dating is built in for anyone who wants it. It's 18+.

Where is Based available?

Based is opening in Calgary first, with new cities opening as membership grows.

How do I get in?

Just sign up in the app with your email. Each city has a member cap, so a city that's full will open more spots as membership grows.

Is Based free?

Yes, Based is free to join and use. An optional Premium membership with extras is coming.

How is Based different from other social media?

Everyone is a real, verified person in your city. There are no follower counts or popularity scores, useful posts reach more people while noise doesn't, and the point is to get you out to events and spots with people you'd actually like. Dating is there too, for anyone who wants it.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not in the App Store yet. On iPhone, open based-social.com/app in Safari and add it to your home screen; it works like an app. Android members can download the app here.
