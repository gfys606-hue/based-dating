The rules are what make it work.

Every dating app says it's different. Based enforces it. The same rules for everyone, and nobody can pay their way around them.

01

Talk, or fade out

Match with as many people as you want. But if you collect matches and never reply, fewer and fewer people see you. Start talking again and you come back.

02

A call within 3 days

Every match gets a quick voice or video call in the app within 3 days. Five minutes is enough to know if there's something there.

03

"Not feeling it" is fine

One tap ends a match politely, and it never counts against you. Being honest is free. Leading someone on isn't.

04

Real photos of real people

Three clear photos of your face, checked against a live selfie. No sunsets, no plates of food, no back-of-the-head mystery shots.
